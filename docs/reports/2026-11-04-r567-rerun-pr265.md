## Where pass@1 is lost

Yan Agent fails pass@1 on 38 (Sonnet) and 48 (Haiku) normal tasks. Python fails 4 and 24 of the same
tasks; 34 (Sonnet) and 24 (Haiku) are Yan-Agent-only losses. Of the Yan Agent failures, 29 / 27 had
checker diagnostics on the first body reply; 9 / 21 did not (3 / 3 ended with `STOP:`, the rest are
hidden-case failures with the model's own tests green, mostly MBPP ambiguity that Python shares).

Tasks whose first body reply hit each cause, grouped by lowering candidate
(`docs/decisions-2026-11-04-r567.md` numbering). A task can hit several. "Any round" counts every
round and both phases.

## Reading

- #265 removed most E210 and `ord`/`chr` rounds: E210 is now 2 tasks per model, all with three or
  more same-typed parameters, and `ord`/`chr` no longer appear as E017. That did not move Sonnet's pass@1.
- Candidate 1 is still the largest single cause: about 40% of Sonnet's and a quarter of Haiku's
  first-try losses. It is decided and unimplemented, so it is the next lever. With candidates 1 and 8
  implemented, at most 20 (Sonnet) and 18 (Haiku) first-try failures would clear; that narrows the
  pass@1 gap to roughly 13 and 6 points if every one then passes, which is an upper bound.
- `chr` returning `char fail` (R801) costs a round when the model writes `chr(n)` without `?`/`else`;
  1 task on Sonnet, 1 on Haiku. A primer line would cover it.
- The primer still tells models to pass two same-typed arguments by keyword; after R800 that line is
  stale and could be shortened.

## Caveats

- One seed, 100 tasks, CLI default temperature. Run-to-run variation on unchanged inputs is about 4
  points (Python arm, Sonnet).
- Both models are Anthropic; no second family.
- Counts by cause come from `classify.py` in the project-files run folder, a regex over diagnostic text.
