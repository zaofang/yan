# Trademark policy

Not legal advice; pending lawyer review. Policy per spec rows R364 and R365.

The project lead claims these marks:

- **Yan**, **言** and the Yan logo (word and logo registration planned in classes 9 and 42).
- The product names **Yan Agent**, **Yan Shell**, **Yan Meta**, **Yan Binary Format** and other "Yan <job>" names (unregistered).

The Apache-2.0 and CC-BY-4.0 licenses cover code and text. They grant no rights in these marks.

## Allowed without asking

- Naming the project accurately: "written in Yan", "a Yan compiler", "works with Yan", "Yan tutorial".
- Package, crate, module or repo names that describe compatibility, such as `yan-foo` or `foo-for-yan`.
- Unmodified copies of the project's own logo when linking to or reporting on the project, with no change to
  shape, colour or proportions.
- Distro packages of Yan, including ones with build or security patches, under the name Yan.

## Not allowed

- Implying endorsement, sponsorship or affiliation by the project or its lead.
- Using Yan or 言 as the name of your fork, product, company, domain, app, or registry namespace when you
  distribute a modified version. Forks must rename. Distro packages with build or security patches are exempt.
- "Yan-compatible" for a version and surface unless the implementation passes the project's published conformance
  suite for that version and surface.
- "Official" or "std" for anything that is not a project artifact. The registry namespaces `yan`, `std`, `core`,
  `yag` and `yn` are reserved to the project.
- Modified, recoloured or stylised logos, or using the logo as your own.
- Confusingly similar names, spellings or characters.
